# Agency-first market entry

Updated 2026-10-10. This applies the owner's supplied research to the current product. Segment demand, prices, savings and conversion rates remain unvalidated. This is an operating plan, not a profitability forecast or enterprise launch approval.

## Offer and conversion path

Focus the first discovery conversations on development agencies with a current client project intended for customer-owned AWS. Founders remain a secondary audience. Validate this segment hypothesis through actual qualified conversations.

The public promise is a source-informed cloud launch plan and a scoped assisted engagement. Do not sell automatic provisioning, guaranteed uptime, measured savings, complete runtime understanding or compliance certification. A reviewed diagram is not a deployable infrastructure plan or a delivered cloud environment.

`/for-agencies` presents the selected-repository/design/request/handover workflow with one primary consultation action. Visitors inquire before granting repository access. Optional business type, timeline and AWS account context, plus source-page attribution, reach the operator inbox. Qualification still requires an operator review; an inquiry is not revenue.

Begin scoping with one client business, selected repositories and one proposed environment. Confirm build behaviour, data sensitivity, traffic, region, availability, source ownership, target date, cloud account ownership and support responsibility. Extra environments, migrations, complex failover and ongoing support need explicit scope and delivery capacity. No fixed public package price has been introduced.

## Quote and margin discipline

Use `/platform-admin/service-economics` before quoting. Enter effort for scoping, architecture review, access setup, implementation, rework, handover and expected support. Enter provider fees, project/AI costs, acquisition, refund allowance and overhead separately. These same-currency inputs are local planning assumptions, not actual revenue or a persisted quote. No exchange-rate or tax assumptions are embedded.

Contribution is service price less payment fees, imputed operator effort, project costs, acquisition and allowance. Price at a chosen target margin accounts for the percentage fee. Monthly capacity uses delivery hours remaining after sales and administration. Unfilled optional fields are treated as zero for arithmetic, not confirmed zero cost. Cash flow excluding founder effort does not establish a sustainable margin.

The research's $1,500–$2,000 initial price is an interview hypothesis. Validate the scope and economics before publishing prices. Record actual delivery time and rework; revise scope or pricing when observed results differ.

## Delivery and acceptance

Each request has a permanent `/dashboard/services/{request_id}` page for progress, reports, replies, a quote/payment link when present, and its saved-design submission snapshot. Notification and report links lead to the specific request.

An owner/admin explicitly accepts the latest report. The audit receipt binds to its ID and checksum. A retry returns the existing receipt. A newer report needs new acceptance; earlier receipts remain in the timeline. The request remains delivered until operations closes it. Closing a request with a report requires acceptance of the latest version. Historical closed records receive no fabricated acceptance.

Acceptance records a customer's review of reported work. It does not independently validate AWS resources, recovery, security or compliance. Publish actual artifacts, limits, unresolved actions and support ownership. Exclude internal browser-review records from customer-success and revenue metrics.

## Validate before scaling acquisition

For the first discovery batch, record each prospect's trigger, alternative, project readiness, access objections, budget discussion and reason for declining. Do not send outreach or run ads automatically. Attribution alone does not prove higher conversion.

Track inquiry → qualified inquiry → agreed scope → verified live payment → actual delivery → latest-report acceptance, with counts and elapsed time. Separately record operator hours, rework, refunds and support. Inquiry qualification and report acceptance are recorded; consolidated revenue/margin analytics are not being claimed.

Before paid acquisition, establish actual delivery capability, reconciled live payments, a recoverable customer launch, tenant/security acceptance and handover evidence. Agree the cohort and spend limit with the owner. Obtain permission before publishing a client's case study or diagram. Internal workflow records do not establish product-market fit.

## Research claims not adopted

- The selected free-only AI routing and Stripe/Razorpay providers remain. No silent paid fallback or provider substitution was enabled. A successful free-model response does not guarantee availability or correctness.
- Competitor prices, compute limits, savings, AWS credit amounts/eligibility, currency rates and conversion benchmarks were not verified for publication and are not used as promises.
- Statements that a merchant of record is universally required or that nearly every web deployment needs the same VPC were not adopted. Payment/tax obligations and workload architecture need separate reviewed decisions.
- Never ask customers to send root credentials, administrative keys or passwords. Review scoped role access separately; observation does not authorize provisioning.
- Existing production gates still apply. A new page, calculator or accepted internal report does not make the platform enterprise-ready.
